Add a snowmaking widget to your site

Show a live card for any resort on your website, blog or ski club page: can they make snow right now, how many of the next 48 hours they can, and how much new snow is coming. It follows NOAA's forecast, refreshed every couple of hours. Free, with no sign-up, no cookies and no tracking.

Good to know

  • The link in the code is part of the widget. Until the card loads, or where scripts are blocked, it's an ordinary link to the resort's forecast.
  • To show several resorts, paste one link per resort. A single script tag covers them all.
  • Some site builders remove script tags. If the card doesn't appear, the link still works; look for an "HTML" or "embed code" block instead.
