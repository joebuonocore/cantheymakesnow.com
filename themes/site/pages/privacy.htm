Privacy

Can They Make Snow? is run by Albright Labs LLC. We keep as little as we can.

Lookups

When you check a place, we count lookups by city and state (not your exact location or address) to show recently checked places on the map. Your current location, if you share it, is only sent to look up the forecast and is not stored.

Saved places and settings

Saved and recent places and your °F/°C choice are kept in your browser only.

Email alerts

If you sign up for alerts we store your email address, the resorts and alert types you chose, your units, and when we last emailed you. Emails are sent through Brevo. We never sell or share your address, and we only email you after you confirm. Every email has a link to change or stop alerts; unsubscribing stops all emails right away. To have your address deleted entirely, email support@albrightlabs.com.

Analytics and maps

We use Google Analytics to count visits and Google Maps for the map and place search; Google's privacy policy applies to those. Forecasts come from NOAA's National Weather Service.
