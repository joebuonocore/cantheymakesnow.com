Early-season snowmaking watch {% partial 'info-tip' label='About the season data' %}

Resorts need cold, dry hours to build a base before natural snow arrives. This ranks every resort by how many hours it has been cold enough to make snow (wet-bulb below {{ (-2)|temp|raw }} at the base or summit) so far this season, plus how many more are forecast for the next 7 days. It says nothing about when a resort will open: that depends on water, crews and how much terrain they cover.
